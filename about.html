ABOUT

Materials science,
across experiment and simulation.

I am Humaun Kabir, a Ph.D. researcher in Materials Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University, working with Professor Hong Liang in the Surface Science Research Group.

My research connects molecular interactions and interfacial chemistry with friction, wear, thermal transport and field-responsive behavior. I combine experimental materials characterization with density functional theory and molecular dynamics to investigate functional materials and fluids.

Before joining Texas A&M, I taught and conducted materials research at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology. My broader publication record also includes functional oxides, 2D materials, computational alloy studies and environmental materials.

TECHNICAL EXPERTISE

Methods & capabilities

Synthesis & surface engineering

Hydrothermal and sol–gel synthesis, spin coating, controlled oxidation, amine and silane functionalization, particle dispersion and formulation.

Thermophysical & tribological testing

Thermal conductivity, rheometry, friction and wear, adhesion, EIS, contact resistance, electrorheology and leakage-current measurements.

Materials characterization

SEM/EDS, XRD, XPS, FTIR, UV–Vis, TGA, 3D profilometry and wettability.

Computational modeling

Quantum ESPRESSO, CASTEP, LAMMPS, ABCluster and xTB; optimization, interfacial energetics, electronic and elastic properties, electrostatics and eHEX thermal transport.
